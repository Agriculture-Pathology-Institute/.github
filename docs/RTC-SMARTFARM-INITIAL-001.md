SECTION 1: LEGAL FRAMEWORK OF THE OFFICIAL SURVEY
-----------------------------------------------------

All initial 3D laser scanner, UAV LiDAR, and RTK-GNSS coordinate networks must map to a legally verified cadastral framework before subgrade excavation or heavy vehicle deploy commences. In the State of Washington, boundary location tracking and layout recording are strictly governed by statutory law to establish chain of title and protect adjoining land parameters:

1.  The Professional Land Surveyors Act (RCW 18.43): Directs that all spatial datums used to govern automated earthmoving equipment (such as the autonomous path-planning grading logic on the Rheinmetall Kodiak AEV) must be compiled under the direct supervision of a licensed Professional Land Surveyor (PLS).
2.  Recording Requirements & Standards (RCW 58.09): Mandates that any survey establishing or redefining land boundaries must be formally filed as a "Record of Survey" with the respective County Auditor within thirty (30) days of monument setting.
3.  Geospatial Reference Datums (RCW 58.20): Dictates the official Washington State Coordinate System. All 3D point cloud meshes converted via the custom Python pipelines into our native 16-state hexadecimal registers must align to either NAD83 (National Spatial Reference System) or WGS84 coordinates to prevent physical property line trespass during deep ditch excavation passes.
4.  Underground Utility Damage Prevention (RCW 19.122): Requires that before any subsurface automated drainage installations can be executed by autonomous machinery, a formal "811 One-Call" lookback ticket must be logged. Actual sensor scans must be verified against public utility geographic maps.
